Our story

Four decades of backing bold genetic science

UK Registered Charity No. 1211119 — a strategic investor in genomic medicine, working where discovery meets the clinic.

Who we are

From a paediatric research unit to the genomics revolution

The Generation Trust was originally founded in 1983 to support medical research at the Paediatric Research Unit at Guy's Hospital. We became a Charitable Incorporated Organisation (CIO) in 2025, and today we work closely with the Department of Medical & Molecular Genetics at King's College London.

We primarily fund pioneering research with real-world clinical applications — improving genetic diagnosis, carrier screening and, potentially, therapeutic interventions within the Genomics Medicine Group (GMG).

Our journey

Milestones along the way

1983

Founded at Guy's Hospital

Registered with the Charity Commission on 18 April 1983 to support genetic research into paediatric conditions at the Paediatric Research Unit.

1990s–2010s

Evolving with genomics

As genomic science advanced, so did our focus — funding gene discovery, diagnosis and the researchers driving the field forward.

2025

Becoming a CIO

We became a Charitable Incorporated Organisation, strengthening our governance for the next generation of funding.

Today

A strategic partner to King's College London

We fund the full trajectory of genomic innovation — from identifying genetic mechanisms to precision clinical tools.

How we work

Lean, responsive and catalytic

A lean structure and close ties to Guy's Hospital let us react quickly to funding requests when timing matters most.

Catalytic grants
