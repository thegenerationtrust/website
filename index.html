Why we exist

A strategic partner at every stage of discovery

From the first identification of a genetic mechanism to precision tools in the clinic, we provide the capital and confidence that lets high-impact science move faster.

01

Supporting promising researchers

We back early-career scientists so they can establish themselves and compete for larger grants — filling a critical gap where less-established researchers so often struggle to secure funding.

02

Agile and flexible funding

With a lean structure and a longstanding, close relationship with Guy's Hospital, we respond rapidly to funding requests — moving at the speed that great research demands.

03

High-impact funding

Beyond direct support, our grants act as a catalyst — helping researchers unlock major institutional and government funding that multiplies every pound we give.

Our mission

Funding the full trajectory of genomic innovation

Founded in 1983 at Guy's Hospital to pioneer genetic research into paediatric conditions, The Generation Trust has evolved alongside the genomics revolution. Today, as a Charitable Incorporated Organisation, we act as a strategic partner to King's College London.

  • ✓Provide the strategic capital that supports the entire research ecosystem.
  • ✓Bridge the journey from identifying genetic mechanisms to clinical precision tools.
  • ✓Ensure high-impact science is supported at every stage of its development.
